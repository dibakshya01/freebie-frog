Try it first

One command. Requires Go 1.24+.

$ go install github.com/dibakshya01/freebie-frog/cmd/freebie@latest

Please read

Responsible use.

Use your own keys, one account per provider. freebie-frog is bring-your-own-key and single-account by design. Do not use it to rotate accounts or pool quota across them — that violates providers' terms (for example, Groq's AUP).

Review each provider's data and privacy terms. Free tiers sometimes train on inputs. Check before sending sensitive or proprietary code, or route it to a local model instead.

Keep it local unless you mean to. It binds loopback by default. Exposing it on a network is opt-in, token-gated, and at your own risk — it is not a multi-tenant security boundary.

Expect early-stage software. It's in active development. Evaluate it before depending on it for anything important, and report what you find.
